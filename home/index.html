Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about forensic document, handwriting, signature and fingerprint examination.

What forensic examination services do you provide?

We provide professional examination and comparison services relating to questioned documents, handwriting, signatures, fingerprints, and suspected alterations or tampering.

How can I know if my signature or handwriting has been forged?

A questioned signature or handwriting sample can be examined against suitable genuine comparison specimens to identify significant similarities, differences, and other relevant characteristics.

Can you examine a document for suspected forgery or tampering?

Yes. Depending on the material available, examination may help identify indications of alterations, erasures, additions, overwriting, substitutions, or other irregularities.

Do I need the original document?

Original documents are generally preferred because they allow better examination of relevant features. However, available scans or copies can initially be reviewed to determine whether examination is feasible.

Can I obtain an expert report for a legal or court matter?

Where appropriate, a professional expert report or opinion can be prepared based on the examination conducted. Court-related requirements can be discussed according to the circumstances of the matter.

Can I consult you before submitting my documents?

Yes. An initial consultation can help determine the type of examination required, the material that may be needed, and the appropriate next steps.

How can I submit my case?

Use the “Submit Your Case” or “Book a Consultation” option to provide the basic details of your matter and available documents/materials for review.

Are my case details and documents kept confidential?

Case information and documents are handled confidentially, subject to applicable professional and legal requirements.
