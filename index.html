INTERNATIONAL FINGERPRINT CARD FORMATS

These formats serve different requesting workflows. Select the exact form named by the receiving authority; do not treat formats as interchangeable.

Current forms, collection methods, identity documents and submission routes are set by the relevant authority and can change. Please verify the latest instructions directly before booking fingerprinting or sending documents.

United States · FBI

FBI Applicant Fingerprint Card — FD-258

FD-258 is a U.S. applicant fingerprint card used when a receiving agency or authorised channel specifically requests this format. Its use does not by itself establish which background-check or identity-history process applies.

Who may need it

Applicants whose U.S. requesting organisation or agency names FD-258 in its instructions. Confirm the recipient, purpose and permitted submission route before fingerprints are taken.

Fingerprinting and documentation

Ask the requesting authority whether rolled and plain impressions are required, the required sequence, who may take the prints, and whether an original ink card or another capture method is accepted. Follow the current FD-258 fields and its instructions; confirm any required identity information, signature, date or consent with the recipient.

Submission guidance

Send the completed card only through the route specified by the requesting authority. Do not substitute FD-1164 or SF-87 unless the recipient explicitly approves that format.

United States · confirm with requester

FBI Fingerprint Card — FD-1164

FD-1164 is a distinct form from FD-258 and SF-87. The exact purpose and acceptance of the form depend on the requesting organisation’s instructions. Confirm that the requester requires the current FD-1164 version and identify the intended recipient before arranging fingerprinting.

Who may need it

Only applicants whose requesting U.S. authority or organisation expressly identifies FD-1164 for their process. Do not infer that a request for “FBI fingerprints” means this form.

Fingerprinting and documentation

Ask the requester and fingerprinting agency which rolled and plain impressions, identifiers, signatures, dates and identity documents are required. Follow the current card’s printed instructions; requirements for FD-258 or SF-87 do not automatically apply.

Submission guidance

Confirm whether the original card, a particular collection provider or a specified mailing/electronic route is required. Obtain confirmation from the requesting authority if the named form or process is unclear.

United States · federal personnel process

Standard Form 87 — SF-87

SF-87 is the U.S. Standard Form titled “Fingerprinting for Federal Civilian Employment.” It is associated with federal civilian personnel processing and should be used only when the responsible federal agency directs the applicant to use it.

Who may need it

Applicants in a federal civilian employment or related agency process where the hiring or sponsoring agency specifically provides or requests SF-87.

Fingerprinting and documentation

Follow the sponsoring agency’s current instructions for the form edition, fingerprint capture, rolled and plain impressions where specified, identifying details and any required agency representative certification. Confirm acceptable identity documents and whether the agency arranges capture.

Submission guidance

Return the form through the hiring or sponsoring agency’s stated process. SF-87 is not a general replacement for applicant cards used in other FBI or international workflows.

Canada · RCMP

RCMP Fingerprint Form — C-216C

C-216C is an RCMP fingerprint form associated with fingerprint-based criminal record processing in Canada. Whether it is the correct form depends on the requested RCMP service and the instructions from the Canadian or foreign authority seeking the result.

Who may need it

Applicants directed to submit fingerprints using the RCMP C-216C form for a specific Canadian criminal record check or related request. Confirm the required service and submission path first.

Fingerprinting and documentation

Use the current C-216C and follow the receiving agency’s directions for rolled and flat impressions, identifying details and collector information. Confirm whether an accredited fingerprinting agency, original card, identity documents or additional consent is required for the particular request.

Submission guidance

Follow the RCMP or requesting agency’s current instructions for who may collect the prints, where the card must be sent, and any required supporting documents. A C-216C request is not interchangeable with U.S. FBI forms.
