Forensic Capabilities

Examination services

Focused assessments of documents, fingerprints, and related evidence.

Questioned Documents (QDE)

Handwriting comparison, simulated/traced forgeries, chemical erasures, overwriting, and optical ink differentiation.

  • • Signature Authentication
  • • Disputed Wills & Deeds
  • • Cheque Fraud Detection

Latent Print Analysis

Latent friction ridge recovery using cyanoacrylate, ninhydrin, ALS lighting, and ACE-V minutiae comparison.

  • • Chemical Print Lifting
  • • Minutiae Ridge Matching
  • • Internal Theft / Fraud Audits

ESDA Indentation Recovery

Revealing invisible indented writing impressions transferred to underlying pages to determine writing sequence.

  • • Non-Destructive Electrostatics
  • • Document Page Sequencing
  • • Origin & Timeline Tracing

FBI / PCC Fingerprinting

Biometric recording on standard fingerprint cards for visa, immigration, and clearance applications.

  • • FBI FD-258 Recording
  • • Police Clearance Certificates
  • • RCMP & Global Embassies

Laboratory Rigor

Tools and examination methods

Examination methods are selected to suit each item, with care taken to preserve evidence and support independent review.

Video Spectral Comparator (VSC)

UV, visible, and IR luminescence for ink differentiation and alteration detection.

High-Precision Stereo Zoom Microscopy

High-magnification analysis of line crossings, pen lifts, and stroke tremors.

Electrostatic Detection Apparatus (ESDA)

Non-chemical recovery of latent indentations on multi-page files.

Evidence handling

Preparing evidence for review

Handle original documents as little as possible and keep them in a clean, dry paper envelope. For digital review, provide a clear, uncompressed scan at 600 DPI or higher.
